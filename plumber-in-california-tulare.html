How a typical visit works

How We Handle Common Plumbing Jobs

Every job starts with an assessment and a clear estimate. Here is how a typical visit works for three of the most common plumbing jobs.

01 Water heater

The problem

No hot water or a leaking water heater

What we did

A licensed technician inspects the unit, explains the repair or replacement options with a written estimate, then installs the replacement and tests it before leaving.

The result

Hot water is restored once the replacement is approved and installed, and the work area is left tidy.

02 Drain clearing

The problem

Slow or blocked drains

What we did

The technician clears the blockage with a drain snake or auger, runs water to check the flow, and inspects the line for repeat blockages.

The result

Drains run clear again, and the technician notes anything that may need attention later.

03 Sewer line repair

The problem

Sewer backups or slow drains across the home

What we did

The technician runs a camera inspection to find the cause — often root intrusion or a damaged section — and explains the repair options, including trenchless repair where it applies.

The result

The line is repaired with as little digging as the job allows, and the repair is tested before the visit ends.
