Local problem patterns

Plumbing problems common across California

Whatever the town, California plumbing fails in familiar ways. In Colton, these five are the ones to watch for.

  • Low pressure from old supply lines — corroded or scaled pipes throttle flow, usually worst at the taps furthest from the main.
  • Sewer blockages from roots and grease — the two most common causes of a main-line backup, and both are preventable with regular attention.
  • Leaks that run up the water bill — a silent toilet leak or a dripping outdoor tap can waste hundreds of litres a day.
  • Outdoor lines damaged by sun and weather — exposed pipework, hose bibs and irrigation connections wear out faster than the pipes inside the walls.
  • Washing machine hoses — rubber supply hoses age and burst without warning, and they are one of the most common causes of indoor flooding.

A yearly check of the heater, the visible pipework and the shut-off valves catches most of these before they become emergencies.
