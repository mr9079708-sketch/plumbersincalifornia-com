Local problem patterns

Plumbing problems common across California

Whatever the town, California plumbing fails in familiar ways. In Temecula, these five are the ones to watch for.

  • Ageing water heaters — a tank heater usually lasts around a decade, and an old unit can fail suddenly, flooding the space around it.
  • Hard water scale — mineral buildup shortens water heater life and clogs fixtures in most of the state.
  • Ageing pipes in older homes — galvanised and cast-iron pipework corrodes from the inside, cutting pressure and tinting water.
  • Tree roots in sewer lines — roots find joints in old clay and cast-iron laterals, and slow drains across the home are the warning sign.
  • Running toilets and dripping taps — small leaks that waste a surprising amount of water and quietly inflate the bill.

A yearly check of the heater, the visible pipework and the shut-off valves catches most of these before they become emergencies.
