how much does a plumber make in california

Explore the salary ranges for plumbers in California, factors influencing earnings, and job opportunities in this comprehensive guide.

P
Pro plumbing CaliforniaOctober 2026 · 3 min read

The average salary for a plumber in California ranges from $50,000 to $100,000 annually, depending on experience and location. This article explores salary ranges, factors influencing earnings, and job opportunities in the plumbing industry.

A plumber in California is a skilled tradesperson who installs and repairs piping systems, fixtures, and appliances. They are essential for both residential and commercial properties, ensuring that water supply and waste systems function correctly.

What is the Average Salary for a Plumber in California?

The average salary for plumbers in California is around $70,000 per year. Entry-level plumbers may start at approximately $50,000, while experienced plumbers can earn upwards of $90,000 to $100,000. According to the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, the hourly wage for plumbers in California averages $33.65, which is significantly higher than the national average.

Factors Influencing Plumber Salaries in California

  • Experience: More experienced plumbers typically command higher salaries.
  • Licensing: Licensed plumbers often earn more than those who are not.
  • Specialization: Plumbers specializing in areas like water heater repair and installation in California may earn higher wages.
  • Type of Employment: Plumbers working for large companies may have different pay scales compared to those who are self-employed.
  • Location: Urban areas with higher living costs tend to offer higher salaries.

Regional Salary Variations for Plumbers in California

In California, plumber salaries vary significantly by region:

  • Los Angeles: Plumbers earn an average of $75,000 annually.
  • San Francisco: The average salary is around $85,000, reflecting the high cost of living.
  • San Diego: Salaries typically range from $60,000 to $80,000.
  • Fresno: Plumbers here earn about $55,000 on average.
  • Sacramento: Average salaries are around $70,000.

Job Outlook and Opportunities for Plumbers in California

The job outlook for plumbers in California remains strong. The demand for skilled plumbers is expected to grow by 11% from 2026 to 2036, driven by the need for new construction and aging infrastructure. Many homeowners in California seek residential plumbing services in California, which creates numerous job opportunities.

Common Mistakes When Hiring a Plumber

Homeowners often make mistakes when hiring a plumber, such as:

  • Not checking credentials and licenses.
  • Choosing based solely on price rather than quality of service.
  • Failing to read reviews or ask for references.
  • Not getting a written estimate before work begins.
