What affects the cost

  • Complexity of the job — a simple washer replacement takes minutes; a hidden leak behind a wall takes diagnosis, access, and repair. More complex work takes more time and expertise, and your estimate will spell that out.
  • Materials needed — a standard part costs far less than a specialist fixture or a full water heater. Your estimate lists the parts included, so you can see exactly what you’re paying for.
  • Service type — installation usually costs more than a repair, because it involves new fixtures, fittings, and testing. A repair reuses what’s already there.
  • Urgency — emergency and after-hours work can cost more, because a technician is dispatched right away, day or night. For non-urgent jobs, booking ahead keeps the cost at the standard rate.
